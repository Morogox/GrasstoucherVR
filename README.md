Grasstocuher VR (AKA Grass Simulator)

This is a simple game all about touching grass. In this game, the player has the goal of touching grass as many times as possible before their completely inevitable total annihilation.

Audio is recommended for the best gameplay experience

Despite its simplicity, Grasstoucher VR can still be qualified as a game; it provides the player with rules, interaction, a goal, and consequences for their actions. Under Schell's definition, the problem-solving component of this game for the player can simply be "How can I increase the amount of grass I touch in the shortest amount of time possible before my inevitable demise?" 
As for Juul's definition of a game, Grasstoucher VR includes:
1. Rules that govern the world (e.g., Click to touch grass; the amount of grass touched affects the state of the game)
2. Measurable outcomes in the amount of grass touched
3. Preferable outcomes (Higher amount of grass touched = better outcome)
4. Effort-demanding action (Click to touch grass)
5. Significance assigned by the player (Whether or not the player wants to touch grass is entirely up to them)
6. Negotiable consequence (obliteration in-game does not equate to obliteration in real life)
Therefore, despite its simple mechanics, the game provides an interactive challenge with defined rules and an objective for the player to pursue.
